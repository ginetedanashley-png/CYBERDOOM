HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap Rock, Paper or Scissors. The computer picks at random.
  • Rock beats Scissors · Scissors beats Paper · Paper beats Rock.
  • Same move = a draw.
  • Classic: play as many rounds as you like.
  • First to 5: the first to win 5 rounds wins the match.
  • Use 🔊 to switch sounds on or off and ↺ RESET to start over.
